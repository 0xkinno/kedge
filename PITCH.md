**20-second hook (spoken).** "Three forty in the morning, off Singapore. Ramon's phone just went over the side. His savings were on it. On every other EVM chain, that money is gone. On Portaldot, his wife and his bosun can bring it back."

**60 seconds, live.** Open `/drill`. Say: "House crew keys are signing this on the live testnet. Lorna hears four words on a call and starts it. Ben approves. Now the chain makes everyone wait; that is the safety delay. His new phone finishes it and brings everything home with one signature." Point at the receipt and open one explorer link.

**30 seconds, proof.** Open `/proof`, filter A3, press Check: "That is a real failed transaction. The chain refused a rescue that came too early. Every attack in our campaign was stopped by the chain or by our checks, and the number of honest rescues we blocked is on the Proof page next to it. We also report where we were wrong: we predicted one error name and it was another."

**Close.** "Every Portaldot account can be recovered this way. No new wallet, no seed handed to a company. Reasons for continued support: first public measurements of Recovery on V3, a drop-in protection flow for any wallet, and a market of 1.89 million seafarers and the families who depend on them."

**Fallback.** If the testnet is slow: open `/proof` and decode a recorded rescue (H1) instead; `docs/drills/` holds two recorded live drills.
